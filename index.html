About the game

Hydro-Electric Simulator (HES) is a Roblox game. You run a hydroelectric power plant: water flow, turbines, and grid demand. Public servers are there for group shifts; private servers are free if you want to practice alone.

The wiki covers startup, ranks, and the rest of the plant. Discord is where reports, appeals, and community talk happen.
